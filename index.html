Daily reminder

The planner only works if you open it. Pick a time, then tap the button. Google Calendar opens with your reminder already filled in. Tap Save and it reminds you every day.

Set my daily reminder
Nudges when you forget

If you have not planned your day by your reminder time, Planniit nudges you on your phone. First at your time, then an hour later, then two hours later.

Nudges work while Planniit is open on your phone, even in the background. If you close it completely, your phone may stop them, so keep the Google Calendar reminder too.

Prefer an alarm? Set a daily alarm on your phone labelled "Plan my day". We never see your reminder time. It stays on your device.

Save or restore my planner

Everything you enter stays on this device. Keep a copy so you never lose it.

Keep your planner safe

Your entries are saved on this device only, so nobody else can see them. If you change phone or clear your phone's website data, they would be lost. Save a copy now and then and you can bring everything back any time.

  • Save my planner: first choose Word, PDF or Excel, then tap the button. Word and Excel files can also bring your planner back later. A PDF is only for reading and printing.
  • Copy my planner: copies it as text so you can paste it into a note or send it to yourself in a message.
  • Bring my planner back: choose your saved Word or Excel file, or paste the copied text, and everything returns.
  • Start over: erases everything in Planniit so you can begin fresh. It cannot be undone.
  • If a download does not start, open Planniit in Chrome or Safari instead of inside Instagram or WhatsApp.

What Planniit is for

Planniit helps you turn good intentions into daily action. You set a 90-day vision / goals, plan your days, see where your time goes, build one skill, plan your money and your monthly budget, and look back every week and every month to see how far you've come.

Your privacy: everything you enter here is saved only on this device, your own phone or computer. We don't have access to it, we can't see it and we never collect it. It is personal to you.

Because it lives on your device, clearing your phone's website data would erase it. Use "Save my planner" at the bottom of the Home page now and then to keep a backup copy.

How it works: four simple habits
Use Planniit like an app

Put Planniit on your home screen so it opens in one tap, just like an app. You do not need an app store. After you have opened it once with internet, it also works with no internet at all.

  • iPhone: open Planniit in Safari, tap the Share icon, then tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android: open Planniit in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  • Computer: in Chrome, look for the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
How the game works

Earn XP every day. Plan your day (+20), finish your tasks (up to +40), learn something (+20), record your time (+10), learn at least as long as you scroll (+10), and track your money (+10).

Level up. Your XP grows into ten ranks, from Stargazer to Legend.

Collect badges. Build streaks, finish reviews and reach goals to unlock them. Missing a day never takes anything away. Just come back.
